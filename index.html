Understanding Quintiles (Q1–Q5)

Quintiles divide a reference distribution into five groups, each representing approximately 20% of that distribution. Select a group to learn more.

Select a quintile to view its meaning.

These are relative index classifications, not percentages of neighbourhood residents. Q4 does not mean 80% of residents experience marginalization. Quintiles are ordinal categories, not continuous measurements; do not average them. Higher scores are not automatically worse or better. Confirm the reference population and scoring direction against the original source documentation.

Understanding Data & Measurements

Understanding Geographic Areas

FSA: The first three characters of a Canadian postal code. Postal areas may cross municipal boundaries.

CSD: A municipality or municipal equivalent used for census geography.

Overall Elgin Region: Regional values supplied in the source dataset. FSA and CSD figures cannot automatically be summed or averaged to create regional totals.

Data Sources

Sources and reporting periods appear with each record where supplied; Click on the “information” icon to learn more about the data’s source. Data may differ by geography, collection period and methodology. Missing and suppressed values are not treated as zero.
